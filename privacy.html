Tsentr

Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Tsentr (Google sign-in name “Openclaw-1”) is a private AI assistant that its owner uses through Telegram. It is not offered to anyone else. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

Data the app accesses

Only the owner's own Google account is connected, and only after the owner grants consent on Google's sign-in screen. The app may access:

  • Google Calendar events — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar
  • Google Drive files and folders — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive
  • Google Docs — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents
  • Google Sheets — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets
  • Google Tasks — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks
  • YouTube channel data, read-only — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
  • the account's email address, to know which account is connected

How the data is used

Google data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests, for example answering “what is on my calendar today?” or summarising a document. The assistant changes something in the Google account — an event, a file, a spreadsheet row or a task — only after the owner explicitly confirms it.

To answer a request, the relevant data may be sent to the AI model provider that powers the assistant (Anthropic, with OpenAI as a backup) and processed under that provider's terms. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's private server. Answers that include Google data can remain in the assistant's conversation history on that server. Nothing is published.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone, except the AI model provider needed to process a request, as described above.

Limited Use

Tsentr's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the app can no longer read or change anything in the account, and the stored tokens stop working.
