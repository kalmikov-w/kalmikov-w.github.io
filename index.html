Tsentr

A private AI assistant in Telegram. Google sign-in name: “Openclaw-1”.

What it does

Tsentr is a personal assistant that its owner talks to in Telegram. When the owner asks, it works with the owner's own Google account:

  • Google Calendar — shows upcoming events and finds free time; creates or moves events only after the owner confirms.
  • Google Drive, Docs and Sheets — finds and reads the owner's files, drafts documents and adds rows to spreadsheets on request.
  • Google Tasks — keeps the owner's to-do list.
  • YouTube — read-only: shows the owner's own channel, videos and comments.

Who can use it

Tsentr is not a public service. It runs on the owner's private server, answers only the owner's Telegram account and is connected to a single Google account — the owner's.
